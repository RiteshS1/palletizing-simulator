# Using the simulator

The simulator has four modes, each on its own page.

| Mode | Shows |
|---|---|
| Greedy layer | The baseline packer filling rows and layers |
| Extreme points | The smarter heuristic, with its candidate points |
| Side by side | Both packers on the same 40 cartons |
| Orders | A shift of customer orders flowing onto pallets for three stores |

## Controls

- **Play / pause** with the orange button or the space bar.
- **Step** one carton forward or back with the arrow buttons or the arrow keys.
- **Home** and **End** jump to the empty or the finished pallet.
- The **conveyor** under the 3D view has one carton per placement: its height is the carton's
  height, its colour is its weight (darker is heavier). Click any carton to jump there.
- Drag to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom, and point at a carton to inspect it.

## Overlays

- **Center of mass:** the blue ball and plumb line. The ring on the deck marks the pallet centre; a
  good stack keeps the ball close to it and low.
- **Support polygon:** the blue shape under the current carton is the convex hull of everything
  holding it up. The orange dot is the carton's centre; it must stay inside.
- **Candidate points** (extreme points only): every corner the algorithm considered for the current
  carton. The orange one is the spot it chose.

## Try other boxes

The form on the right runs the **C++ engine compiled to WebAssembly** inside a background worker, so
the page never freezes. Change the number of boxes, the random seed or the minimum support and pack
again. The same seed always gives the same cartons, so you can compare runs fairly.

## Reading the numbers

- **Pallet filled:** carton volume ÷ the pallet's full volume (1200 × 1000 × 1800 mm).
- **Density:** carton volume ÷ (deck area × actual stack height): how much air is inside the stack.
- **Center of mass off-center:** horizontal distance of the center of mass from the pallet centre.
  Lower is safer to wrap and ship.
