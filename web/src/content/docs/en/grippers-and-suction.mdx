# Grippers and suction

The **end-effector** is the part of the robot that touches the product. For cartons, the workhorse is
the **vacuum gripper**: a plate of suction cups connected to a vacuum source.

## How suction holds a carton

A cup sealed on a surface has lower pressure inside than the air outside. The holding force is that
pressure difference times the sealed area:

```text
F = ΔP × A × n
```

with ΔP the vacuum level (Pa), A the area of one cup (m²) and n the number of cups touching the carton.

**Worked example.** Four cups of 40 mm diameter (A ≈ 0.00126 m² each) at ΔP = 60 kPa:
F ≈ 60 000 × 0.00126 × 4 ≈ **302 N**, enough to hold about 30 kg statically. Real designs apply a
**safety factor** of 2 or more, because the robot accelerates: a vertical acceleration of 5 m/s² adds
50% to the force needed, and a fast horizontal move tries to peel the carton off sideways.

## Why it sometimes fails

- **Porous or recycled board** leaks air: the vacuum generator cannot hold ΔP.
- **Tape seams, labels and bulging lids** break the seal of the cups that sit on them.
- **Overhanging cups** on a small carton suck air instead of cardboard. Grippers solve this with
  **zones**: groups of cups switched separately, so only the cups over the carton turn on.

## Sensing the grip

A pressure sensor per zone confirms a good seal before the robot lifts, and watches during the move.
If the vacuum drops, the robot stops before the carton falls: a simple sensor that prevents most dropped loads.

## Other end-effectors

| Gripper | When |
|---|---|
| Vacuum (cups or foam pad) | most cartons, fast, simple |
| Fork or clamp | heavy, open or very porous cases |
| Layer gripper | lifting a whole layer of identical cartons at once |
| Fingered / adaptive | bags, irregular items |

## In this project

The simulator's gripper is a vacuum plate with four cups, and the planner's **reachability** rule
exists because of it: a top-down suction gripper cannot slide a carton under an overhang. The live
model shows the airflow into the cups while vacuum is on.
